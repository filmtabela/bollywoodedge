A quality facial essence is a game-changer for recreating her fresh-faced glow. Look for Korean beauty essences available on Amazon that contain hyaluronic acid and niacinamide—these ingredients plump the skin and create that lit-from-within radiance Sara is known for. Apply the essence after cleansing and before your moisturizer to lock in maximum hydration. Tinted Moisturizer for Natural Coverage

Sara rarely appears with heavy foundation, preferring instead a barely-there base that lets her natural skin shine through. A lightweight tinted moisturizer with SPF is the perfect product to achieve her signature no-makeup look. When shopping on Amazon for tinted moisturizers, search for formulas that offer buildable coverage and sun protection. Cream Blush for a Youthful Flush

Sara's peachy, flushed cheeks give her that youthful, healthy appearance that fans adore. Cream blushes are superior to powder formulas for achieving this dewy, natural-looking flush. When browsing Amazon beauty products, choose cream blushes in coral, peach, or rosy pink shades that complement your skin tone.
