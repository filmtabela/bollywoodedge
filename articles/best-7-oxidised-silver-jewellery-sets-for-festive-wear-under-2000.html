Buying Tips

  • Check for secure clasps and earring backs; oxidised jewellery findings can sometimes be fragile, so ensure hooks and closures are sturdy before purchasing
  • Store pieces separately in ziplock bags with silica gel to prevent tarnishing and scratches; oxidised silver naturally darkens over time but proper storage slows this process
  • Verify the base metal composition if you have skin sensitivities; German silver and brass are common bases but can cause reactions in some people
  • Avoid contact with water, perfumes, and cosmetics as these accelerate tarnishing; always wear jewellery as the last step when dressing up
