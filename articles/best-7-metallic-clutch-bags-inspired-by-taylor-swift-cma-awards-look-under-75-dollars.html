Buying Tips

  • Check interior lining quality and stitching—even budget clutches should have smooth, snag-free interiors that won't damage your phone or belongings
  • Measure your essentials before buying; most metallic clutches are compact, so ensure your phone, keys, and cards will fit comfortably
  • Test closures like magnetic snaps or clasps—they should feel secure and substantial, not flimsy or loose
  • Look for detachable chain straps for versatility, and verify the return policy since metallic finishes can photograph differently than they appear in person
