Buying Tips

  • Check the Chanderi silk percentage in the fabric blend—authentic Chanderi should have at least 60-70% silk for that signature sheen and drape
  • Always verify size charts carefully as Chanderi kurtas often run slim; consider ordering one size up if between sizes
  • Examine stitching quality around embroidery and borders, as loose threads can affect the garment's longevity
  • Confirm the return and exchange policy before purchase, especially for festive wear bought online, and check delivery timelines before Diwali
