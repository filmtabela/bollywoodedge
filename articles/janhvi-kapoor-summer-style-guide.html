The Floral Maxi Dress: Janhvi's Go-To Summer Essential

Janhvi Kapoor frequently gravitates toward flowing maxi dresses adorned with floral prints during the summer months. These feminine, romantic pieces have become her signature off-duty look. To recreate her aesthetic, look for maxi dresses in lightweight cotton or breathable linen blends featuring bold botanical prints. The key is finding styles with flattering wrap silhouettes or empire waistlines that create an elongated, elegant profile while keeping you comfortable in the heat. Amazon offers an extensive collection of floral maxi dresses from both Indian and international brands, making it easy to find pieces that capture Janhvi's breezy sophistication. Opt for dresses with adjustable straps and flexible sizing for the most flattering fit, and don't shy away from vibrant colour palettes—Janhvi often wears bright yellows, coral pinks, and tropical greens that epitomise summer energy.

Crop Tops and High-Waisted Bottoms: Her Fitness-to-Fashion Formula

Known for her dedication to fitness, Janhvi Kapoor masterfully translates her gym-honed style into everyday fashion. Her favourite summer combination pairs fitted crop tops with high-waisted trousers or denim shorts. This silhouette-flattering ensemble showcases her toned physique while remaining practical for India's sweltering summers. When shopping on Amazon for similar pieces, search for ribbed crop tops in neutral tones or pastel shades—these versatile basics can be dressed up with statement jewellery or kept minimal for casual outings. The beauty of this combination lies in its versatility; swap the trousers for high-waisted denim shorts and comfortable sneakers for a more relaxed weekend aesthetic.

No Janhvi Kapoor summer ensemble is complete without oversized, fashion-forward sunglasses. Amazon's vast sunglasses collection includes affordable options that mirror designer styles frequently worn by the actress. Breezy Co-Ord Sets: Effortless Coordination

Janhvi has championed the co-ord set trend, particularly during summer months when getting dressed should be effortless yet stylish. These matching two-piece outfits eliminate the guesswork from styling while maintaining a put-together appearance. Amazon features countless co-ord set options ranging from printed cotton sets perfect for daytime activities to more structured linen blends suitable for evening events. Minimalist Jewellery: The Finishing Touch

These understated accessories add polish without the heaviness of elaborate jewellery—a practical consideration for India's humid summers.
