# Janhvi Kapoor Summer Style Guide: Channel Bollywood Glamour This Season

When it comes to effortless summer style that perfectly balances traditional Indian aesthetics with contemporary fashion sensibilities, Janhvi Kapoor has mastered the art. The young Bollywood sensation has consistently delivered stunning summer looks that range from breezy ethnic wear to chic Western ensembles, making her a style icon for fashion-conscious millennials and Gen-Z audiences alike. Whether she's spotted at the airport in comfortable casuals or attending promotional events in statement pieces, Janhvi's summer wardrobe offers endless inspiration for those looking to elevate their warm-weather fashion game.

This summer style guide breaks down Janhvi's signature looks and shows you how to recreate her most iconic warm-weather outfits with easily accessible pieces that won't break the bank.

The Breezy Cotton Kurta Set: Janhvi's Go-To Casual Look

Janhvi Kapoor frequently embraces traditional Indian cotton kurta sets during summer months, proving that ethnic wear can be both comfortable and stylish in sweltering heat. Her preference for pastel shades and lightweight fabrics makes this a perfect addition to your summer wardrobe. Look for breathable cotton or cotton-blend kurta sets in soft pinks, mint greens, or classic whites with delicate embroidery or minimal prints. These versatile pieces work perfectly for casual outings, work-from-home days, or even dressed up for festive occasions. Pair with comfortable juttis or kolhapuris to complete the look exactly as Janhvi does.

Flowy Maxi Dresses: Airport Style Perfection

The actress's airport looks have become legendary, and her collection of flowy maxi dresses deserves special mention. Janhvi often opts for comfortable, ankle-length dresses in floral prints or solid jewel tones that offer both style and ease during travel. When shopping for similar pieces online, search for breathable fabrics like rayon, cotton, or linen-blend materials that keep you cool while looking effortlessly put-together. Choose maxi dresses with adjustable straps, empire waistlines, or wrap silhouettes that flatter multiple body types. These dresses transition seamlessly from day to evening, making them excellent investment pieces for your summer collection.

High-Waisted Denim Shorts: Western Casual Done Right

For her more Western-inspired summer looks, Janhvi Kapoor gravitates toward high-waisted denim shorts paired with crop tops or tucked-in tank tops. This combination elongates the legs while maintaining a youthful, relaxed vibe perfect for summer outings. Look for medium-wash or light-wash denim shorts with minimal distressing for a cleaner aesthetic that matches Janhvi's style preferences. The high-waisted cut not only flatters the figure but also offers more coverage and comfort than traditional low-rise styles. Pair these with statement sunglasses and minimalist jewelry to capture her signature look.

Statement Sunglasses: The Ultimate Summer Accessory

No Janhvi Kapoor-inspired summer wardrobe would be complete without oversized or uniquely shaped sunglasses. The actress frequently sports everything from classic aviators to trendy cat-eye frames and contemporary geometric shapes. Invest in quality sunglasses with proper UV protection that shield your eyes while elevating any outfit instantly. Choose frames that complement your face shape—round frames for angular faces, or angular frames for rounder face shapes. Sunglasses with gradient lenses in brown, grey, or trendy colored tints can add that celebrity polish to even the simplest summer ensemble.

Lightweight Tote Bags: Practical Meets Fashionable

Janhvi's summer accessories always include practical yet stylish tote bags that accommodate the essentials while maintaining her polished appearance. Canvas totes, woven straw bags, or structured leather alternatives in neutral colors work beautifully for summer. Look for medium to large-sized bags with sturdy straps and internal pockets for organization. These versatile pieces complement both ethnic and Western outfits, making them worthy additions to your collection. Choose colors like tan, beige, white, or soft pastels that coordinate with multiple outfits throughout the season.
