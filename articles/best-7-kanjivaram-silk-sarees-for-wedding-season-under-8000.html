Buying Tips

  • Check for pure silk by doing a burn test on a loose thread—authentic silk burns slowly and smells like burnt hair, while synthetic alternatives melt and smell like plastic
  • Examine the zari quality by looking for real or tested zari weight; genuine silver or copper-coated zari adds value and longevity to your investment
  • Verify the fall and finish of the saree—Kanjivaram silk should feel substantial (400-500 grams), have a crisp texture, and produce a rustling sound when draped
  • Always ask for certification or authenticity marks like Silk Mark when buying, and prefer sellers offering exchange or return policies for online purchases
