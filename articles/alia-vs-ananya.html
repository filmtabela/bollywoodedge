Two generations of Bollywood style, two completely different aesthetics. Alia Bhatt brings quiet luxury and understated elegance — think earthy tones, clean silhouettes and minimal accessories. Ananya Panday brings Gen-Z energy — colour, crop tops, bold prints and the kind of confidence that makes everything look intentional.

Both work. Both are shoppable on Amazon India. The question is: which lane are you?

Alia's Signature Pieces

Alia's street style formula is built on a few key staples — a well-cut kurta set or linen co-ord, straight-leg jeans in a muted wash, and a structured bag. She keeps jewellery minimal — a delicate gold chain at most. The overall effect is polished without looking like she tried.

On Amazon India you can find the closest matches in linen blend kurta sets, straight-leg mid-rise jeans and clean canvas totes that nail her look at a fraction of the price.

Ananya's Signature Pieces

Ananya's approach is the opposite of minimal. She embraces colour — lime green, hot pink, cobalt blue — and pairs bold crop tops with high-waist wide-leg trousers or matching co-ords. Her accessory game is heavy: chunky earrings, layered necklaces and sunglasses that make a statement from across the street.

The good news: this look is entirely achievable on Amazon India. Bold co-ord sets, statement earrings and colourful tops are all well-stocked and affordable.

The Verdict

If you are building a wardrobe that works across multiple occasions — work, events, casual outings — Alia's quiet luxury approach is the smarter investment. If you are dressing for Instagram, a college farewell, or simply want to have more fun with fashion, Ananya's bold aesthetic delivers better results.

The best wardrobes steal from both. Start with Alia's neutral base pieces and add Ananya's bold accessories. That is the real formula.
