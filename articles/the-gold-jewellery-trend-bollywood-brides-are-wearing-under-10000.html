When Alia Bhatt stepped out in her wedding festivities adorned in traditional gold jewellery that perfectly balanced grandeur with contemporary elegance, she set a trend that every style-conscious bride is now rushing to recreate. The best part? You don't need a Bollywood budget to achieve this look. The golden girl aesthetic that Bollywood brides like Katrina Kaif, Kiara Advani, and Alia Bhatt have championed isn't just about dripping in yellow metal—it's about choosing the right pieces that make a statement while maintaining versatility for multiple wedding functions. Whether you're shopping for your mehendi, sangeet, or even the main ceremony, these affordable Amazon finds prove that celebrity style is more accessible than ever.

Temple Jewellery: The Timeless Favourite

South Indian temple jewellery has experienced a massive resurgence, with celebrities pairing these intricate designs with both traditional sarees and contemporary ensembles. The beauty of temple jewellery lies in its versatility—a stunning temple necklace set featuring goddess motifs and antique gold finish can elevate everything from a silk saree to a festive kurti set. Look for sets that include matching earrings and a maang tikka for a cohesive bridal look. These pieces typically range between ₹2,500-₹7,000 on Amazon, offering incredible value for the level of craftsmanship you receive.

The key to nailing this trend is choosing pieces with intricate kemp stone work or ruby-green stone combinations that add colour contrast to your bridal outfit. Celebrity stylists often recommend temple jewellery for daytime functions because it photographs beautifully without looking too heavy.

Polki-Style Chokers: Modern Royalty

Deepika Padukone's wedding looks introduced many brides to the charm of layered Polki jewellery, particularly the statement choker. While authentic Polki runs into lakhs, Amazon offers stunning replicas with similar aesthetics under ₹5,000. A Polki-inspired choker with matching jhumkas creates an instantly regal look perfect for your wedding day or reception.

When shopping for Polki-style pieces online, prioritize sellers with high ratings and detailed product images showing the clasp quality and stone settings. The best affordable options feature a gold-plated brass base with cubic zirconia or synthetic stones that mimic the Polki effect beautifully.

Layered Rani Haar: Maximum Impact

Nothing says Bollywood bride quite like a multi-layered Rani haar cascading elegantly down the neckline. This style staple has been spotted on everyone from Priyanka Chopra to Anushka Sharma during their wedding festivities. The contemporary twist? Modern brides are opting for lighter-weight versions that don't compromise on visual impact.

Amazon's best-selling Rani haar sets typically include three to five layers of gold-plated beads, often accented with kundan or pearl work, priced between ₹3,000-₹8,000. These sets usually come complete with matching earrings and sometimes a maang tikka, giving you excellent value. The layered effect works particularly well with simpler blouse designs, letting the jewellery become the focal point of your look.

Statement Jhumkas: The Celebrity Essential

From red carpet appearances to intimate wedding ceremonies, oversized jhumkas remain the go-to choice for Bollywood's leading ladies. Sonam Kapoor Ahuja has particularly championed the traditional jhumka revival, often pairing them with contemporary outfits for maximum impact.

Look for jhumkas with detailed bell work, pearl drops, or coloured stone embellishments in the ₹1,500-₹4,000 range. The beauty of investing in quality jhumkas is their versatility—they transition seamlessly from wedding functions to festive occasions throughout the year.

Maang Tikka With A Modern Edge

The maang tikka has evolved from a simple forehead ornament to a statement piece that can define your entire bridal look. Alia's minimalist approach to this accessory, choosing sleeker designs over heavily ornate ones, has resonated with millennial brides seeking sophistication over excess.

Contemporary maang tikkas available under ₹3,000 feature everything from geometric patterns to floral motifs with attached passa elements for added drama. These pieces work beautifully whether you're wearing your hair in a traditional bun or loose waves.
