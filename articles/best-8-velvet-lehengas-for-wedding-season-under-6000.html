Buying Tips

  • Check velvet quality by feeling its weight and texture—good velvet should feel substantial but not stiff, with consistent pile throughout the fabric
  • Always verify the blouse and lehenga measurements carefully, especially for semi-stitched pieces, and confirm customization options with sellers before ordering
  • Look for dry-clean-only labels and factor in maintenance costs; velvet requires special care to maintain its texture and sheen over time
  • Read return policies thoroughly as many ethnic wear items are non-returnable once altered, and order at least 3-4 weeks before your event for any customization
