Buying Tips

  • Check fabric composition carefully—pure silk will mention 'pure silk' or specific types like katan or tussar, while blends should clearly state percentages to avoid misleading descriptions
  • Examine zari quality by looking for terms like 'pure zari' or 'tested zari' in descriptions; avoid vague terms, and note that real gold/silver zari will push prices higher
  • Verify return and exchange policies before purchasing, especially for sarees, as color variations between screen and actual product are common with traditional weaves
  • Read recent reviews focusing on fabric quality, color accuracy, and blouse piece inclusion—some sellers skip the blouse or provide mismatched pieces
