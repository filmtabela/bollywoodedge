Buying Tips

  • Check sequin attachment quality—gently tug a few sequins to ensure they're securely stitched rather than glued, which can come off after dry cleaning
  • Order one size larger than usual if choosing net or lycra blouses, as sequin embellishment reduces fabric stretch and can feel restrictive
  • Read care instructions carefully—most sequin sarees require professional dry cleaning only, and improper washing will damage the embellishments permanently
  • Verify return policies for embellished sarees as many sellers don't accept returns once the fall and pico stitching is done or if worn even briefly
