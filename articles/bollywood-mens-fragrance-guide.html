This powerhouse fragrance has become a staple among Bollywood's A-listers for good reason. Dior Sauvage combines fresh bergamot with spicy pepper and earthy ambroxan, creating a scent that's both modern and timeless. Perfect for the man who wants to channel Hrithik Roshan's refined masculinity, this men's eau de toilette works beautifully from day to night. The longevity is exceptional, meaning a few sprays in the morning will carry you through evening events. When Bollywood stars want to make a statement, they reach for Creed Aventus. This iconic men's luxury perfume has achieved cult status globally, with its distinctive blend of pineapple, birch, and musk creating an unforgettable olfactory signature. ### Tom Ford Oud Wood Eau de Parfum

For those seeking something more unique and exotic, Tom Ford Oud Wood perfectly captures the east-meets-west sensibility that defines modern Bollywood style. This men's oriental fragrance features rare oud wood blended with exotic spices and vanilla, creating a warm, sophisticated scent profile. It's perfect for evening wear and special occasions when you want to stand out. The understated luxury of this men's designer fragrance appeals to those with refined tastes who appreciate subtle complexity over loud projection.

### Versace Eros Flame for Men

Capturing youthful energy with mature sophistication, Versace Eros Flame is ideal for the contemporary Bollywood fan who follows stars like Vicky Kaushal or Kartik Aaryan. A modern classic that's found favor among Bollywood's sophisticated leading men, Acqua di Giò Profumo reimagines the original with added depth and intensity.
