The Foundation: Getting Your Denim Right

The entire look hinges on finding the perfect pair of baggy jeans. Stars like Hailey Bieber and Gigi Hadid have made wide-leg baggy high-waist jeans their go-to travel essential, and for good reason. These jeans offer the coverage and comfort needed for long flights while creating that coveted relaxed silhouette that photographs beautifully.

The key is choosing jeans with a high waist to maintain structure and prevent the look from appearing sloppy. Look for styles in classic blue denim or versatile black – both work seamlessly with various top options. The wide-leg cut provides room to move while the high-waist design elongates your legs, creating a balanced proportion that's both flattering and comfortable for hours of travel.

Building the Perfect Airport Outfit

Once you've secured your ideal baggy jeans, the rest of the outfit comes together naturally. Celebrities typically pair their relaxed denim with fitted or cropped tops to maintain that all-important balance. Think along the lines of a simple white tank top, a fitted crop tee, or even a sleek bodysuit – anything that creates contrast with the volume of the jeans.

For layering, a structured blazer or oversized leather jacket adds instant sophistication. This combination is spotted repeatedly on style icons like Deepika Padukone and Priyanka Chopra Jonas during their airport appearances. The juxtaposition of tailored outerwear with casual denim creates an elevated yet approachable aesthetic that works whether you're travelling business class or economy.

Accessories That Complete the Look

The right accessories transform a basic outfit into a celebrity-worthy ensemble. A quality tote or shoulder handbag becomes essential for airport travel – it needs to be spacious enough for your essentials while complementing your overall aesthetic. Opt for structured leather or vegan leather designs in neutral tones like tan, black, or cognac brown that coordinate with multiple outfits.

Beyond your handbag, consider these style-elevating additions: oversized sunglasses for that instant celebrity disguise effect, minimal gold jewellery for subtle sophistication, and pristine white sneakers or comfortable loafers for practical footwear. A sleek baseball cap or beanie can add personality while keeping bad hair days at bay during travel.
