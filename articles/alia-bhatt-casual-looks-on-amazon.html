Essential Pieces to Recreate Alia's Casual Wardrobe

**Classic White Oversized Shirt**

Alia is frequently photographed in crisp white oversized shirts, often paired with cycling shorts or distressed denim. An oversized white cotton shirt is the ultimate wardrobe staple that works for everything from brunch dates to casual office Fridays. Look for pure cotton options with good quality stitching that won't lose shape after washing. Style it half-tucked into high-waisted jeans or knot it at the waist for a more fitted silhouette. Amazon offers numerous options from brands like Levi's, GAP, and Indian labels that deliver both quality and affordability.

**High-Waisted Mom Jeans**

The actress has single-handedly brought back the mom jeans trend in India. Her preference for light-wash, high-waisted denim with a relaxed fit perfectly balances comfort and style. These jeans elongate your legs while providing room to breathe—unlike the restrictive skinny jeans of the previous decade. Pair them with crop tops, tucked-in tees, or oversized blazers for multiple looks. When shopping on Amazon, check customer reviews for accurate sizing information and look for jeans with at least 2% elastane for better fit and comfort.

**Minimalist Sneakers**

Alia rarely steps out without her beloved white sneakers. Clean, minimal sneakers in white or neutral tones are the foundation of her casual footwear collection. These versatile shoes work with everything from dresses to joggers and instantly give any outfit a contemporary, youthful vibe. Brands like Puma, Adidas, and Nike offer excellent options on Amazon with reliable delivery and easy returns. Choose leather or premium synthetic materials that are easy to clean and maintain their fresh appearance.

**Comfortable Athleisure Sets**

As a fitness enthusiast, Alia is often spotted in coordinated athleisure sets featuring sports bras, high-waisted leggings, and matching jackets. These sets aren't just for the gym—they've become acceptable casual wear for running errands, traveling, or relaxing at home. Look for moisture-wicking fabrics with four-way stretch that provide support without feeling restrictive. Amazon's athleisure section features both international brands and affordable Indian alternatives that deliver comparable quality at lower price points.

**Statement Sunglasses**

While Alia keeps most accessories minimal, she never skips sunglasses. Her collection includes classic aviators, oversized frames, and trendy cat-eye styles. A good pair of sunglasses instantly elevates the simplest outfit while protecting your eyes. When shopping for sunglasses on Amazon, prioritize UV protection over just aesthetics. Look for polarized lenses that reduce glare and provide genuine sun protection, not just tinted fashion accessories.
