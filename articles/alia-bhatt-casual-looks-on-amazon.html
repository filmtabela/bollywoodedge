Essential Pieces to Recreate Alia's Casual Wardrobe

### White Oversized Shirt

No casual wardrobe is complete without a crisp white oversized shirt, and Alia has been photographed wearing this staple countless times. Look for cotton or linen blend shirts on Amazon that hit mid-thigh and feature a relaxed fit through the shoulders. Style it like Alia by pairing it with high-waisted denim shorts or cycling shorts, leaving a few buttons undone for that effortlessly cool vibe. The beauty of this piece lies in its versatility—wear it as a beach cover-up, knot it at the waist over jeans, or layer it over a slip dress for added dimension.

### High-Waisted Denim Shorts

Alia frequently sports well-fitted denim shorts during Mumbai's sweltering summers, and they've become her go-to for casual outings. When shopping on Amazon, opt for medium to light wash shorts with a high-waisted cut that hits just above the belly button. Look for shorts with a slight distressed detail or frayed hem to add that contemporary edge Alia loves.
