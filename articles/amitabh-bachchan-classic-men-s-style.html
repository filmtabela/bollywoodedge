His signature look combines impeccably tailored pieces, statement accessories, and an innate understanding of what works for his towering frame. Whether you're dressing for a formal occasion or want to elevate your everyday wardrobe, channeling Amitabh Bachchan's classic style is a masterclass in timeless menswear.
