Buying Tips

  • Check velvet quality by testing the pile—good velvet should feel dense and soft, not sparse or scratchy
  • Always check blouse piece fabric and length; velvet sarees often come with matching or contrast blouse material
  • Read return policies carefully as velvet shows creases easily and may look different once unfolded
  • Store velvet sarees on padded hangers or rolled (never folded) to prevent permanent crush marks on the pile
