Buying Tips

  • Check the fabric composition carefully—pure wool and pashmina blends offer better warmth than acrylic or polyester, though they may require dry cleaning
  • Examine embroidery quality by checking if threads are secure and patterns are symmetrical; loose threads indicate poor craftsmanship that won't last
  • Consider the shawl's dimensions—larger shawls (100x200cm) offer more draping options, while stoles (70x200cm) are easier to manage during functions
  • Verify the return policy before purchasing, especially for online orders, as colours and fabric texture can appear different in person than in photographs
