Buying Tips

  • Check fabric composition carefully—pure silk may need dry cleaning while blends are often machine-washable, affecting long-term costs
  • Examine embroidery and zari work in product photos for loose threads or uneven patterns before purchasing online
  • Always order blouse pieces unstitched if measurements vary from standard sizing, and budget extra for tailoring
  • Read return policies thoroughly as many sarees, especially those with embellishments, may not be eligible for returns once the blouse is stitched
