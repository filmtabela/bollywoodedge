Why Denim Jackets Are Having a Major Moment

The denim jacket has evolved from a Western workwear staple to a fashion-forward layering essential that transcends seasons. What makes it particularly appealing for Indian weather is its adaptability—light enough for Mumbai's mild winters yet substantial enough to elevate any casual outfit. Bollywood celebrities have been spotted wearing denim jackets everywhere from film promotions to casual coffee runs, making it clear this trend isn't going anywhere.

The current iteration of this trend focuses on relaxed fits, vintage washes, and strategic layering. Unlike the fitted styles of the early 2000s, today's denim jackets for men embrace a more lived-in aesthetic with slightly oversized cuts that allow for comfortable movement while maintaining that coveted cool-guy vibe.

The Perfect Foundation: Nailing the Bottom Half

The secret to mastering the denim jacket trend lies in balancing proportions. When you're working with a structured top layer, your bottom half needs careful consideration. This is where wide-leg baggy high-waist jeans become your style saviour. Priced between ₹899 and ₹1,999, these affordable jeans deliver that effortlessly cool silhouette seen on Bollywood's most stylish men. The high waist creates a flattering line when worn with cropped denim jackets, while the relaxed leg opening adds contemporary edge to your ensemble.

Ayushmann Khurrana recently demonstrated this pairing perfectly at a recent event, showing how the contrast between a fitted jacket and wider trousers creates visual interest without overwhelming your frame.

Styling Tips from Bollywood's Best-Dressed Men

The key to avoiding the Canadian tuxedo effect is playing with contrast. Pair darker denim jackets with lighter jeans or vice versa. Alternatively, break up the denim entirely by incorporating chinos, cargo pants, or those wide-leg baggy high-waist jeans in neutral colours like beige, olive, or black.

Layering is where the magic happens. Try your denim jacket over hoodies, sweatshirts, or even over shirts for a rugged aesthetic. Sidharth Malhotra recently demonstrated this at a promotional event, wearing his denim jacket casually draped over his shoulders for an effortlessly cool vibe.
