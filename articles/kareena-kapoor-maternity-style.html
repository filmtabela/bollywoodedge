# Kareena Kapoor Maternity Style: Your Ultimate Guide to Pregnancy Fashion Like Bebo

When Kareena Kapoor Khan announced her first pregnancy in 2016, the Bollywood fashionista didn't just redefine maternity wear – she revolutionised it entirely. Known affectionately as Bebo, Kareena continued to grace magazine covers, walk ramps, and attend glamorous events, proving that pregnancy is no reason to compromise on style. During both her pregnancies with Taimur and Jeh, she became the ultimate maternity fashion icon, inspiring millions of Indian women to embrace their changing bodies with confidence and panache.

From body-hugging maxi dresses to structured kaftans and chic athleisure, Kareena's maternity wardrobe was a masterclass in comfort meeting couture. Whether you're expecting your first child or already a seasoned mother, recreating Kareena's effortlessly elegant maternity style is easier than you think. Let's explore how you can channel your inner Bebo throughout your pregnancy journey.

The Maxi Dress Magic

Kareena's love affair with maxi dresses during pregnancy is well-documented. She was frequently spotted in flowing, floor-length dresses that gracefully accommodated her baby bump while keeping her look sophisticated and feminine. The beauty of maxi dresses lies in their versatility – they work equally well for casual outings, dinner dates, and festive occasions.

For your maternity wardrobe, invest in a comfortable cotton maxi dress with empire waistline that sits just below the bust. This silhouette flatters your bump beautifully while providing room for growth throughout your pregnancy. Look for breathable fabrics with a bit of stretch, preferably in solid colours or subtle prints that won't overwhelm your frame. Pair these with statement earrings and sandals for an instantly elevated look.

Kaftan Elegance for Every Occasion

Kareena championed the kaftan as the ultimate pregnancy essential, wearing everything from casual printed versions for airport looks to heavily embellished designer pieces for special events. The kaftan's loose, flowing structure makes it perfect for expectant mothers who want to look polished without feeling constricted.

A printed kaftan kurta in vibrant colours can become your go-to outfit for everything from lunch with friends to family functions. Choose one with beautiful traditional prints or modern geometric patterns. The best kaftans have side slits for easy movement and are made from natural fabrics that keep you cool in India's warm climate. Style them with leggings or palazzos for a complete ethnic look.

The Power of Comfortable Leggings

No maternity wardrobe is complete without high-quality leggings, and Kareena knew this well. She regularly paired leggings with long kurtas, oversized shirts, and even blazers for a contemporary fusion look that worked everywhere from gym sessions to casual meetings.

Invest in maternity leggings with adjustable waistbands that grow with your bump. These aren't your regular leggings – they feature specially designed panels that provide support without squeezing. A pack of premium maternity leggings in basic colours like black, grey, and navy ensures you always have comfortable bottoms to pair with your tunics and kurtas. The four-way stretch fabric should offer compression while remaining breathable.

Athleisure with Attitude

True to her fitness-conscious image, Kareena made maternity athleisure look absolutely aspirational. She was photographed countless times leaving her gym sessions in coordinated sports bras and track pants, inspiring pregnant women everywhere to stay active and stylish simultaneously.

A maternity tracksuit set in soft cotton blend fabric is essential for expectant mothers who want to maintain their fitness routine or simply run errands in comfort. Look for sets with adjustable drawstrings and moisture-wicking properties. The coordinated look eliminates the stress of outfit planning while ensuring you look put-together even on your most exhausted days.

The Statement Tote Bag

Kareena understood that accessories make the outfit, and she was never without a spacious, stylish tote bag that accommodated all her pregnancy essentials without compromising on aesthetics.

A large canvas tote bag with multiple pockets becomes incredibly practical during pregnancy and beyond. Choose one in a neutral shade that matches everything in your wardrobe. The roomy interior should comfortably hold your water bottle, snacks, pregnancy documents, and daily essentials.
