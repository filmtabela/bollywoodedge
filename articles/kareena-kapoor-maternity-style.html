# Kareena Kapoor Maternity Style: How Bebo Redefined Pregnancy Fashion

When Kareena Kapoor Khan stepped out flaunting her baby bump with unmatched confidence during both her pregnancies, she didn't just break Bollywood stereotypes—she created a whole new fashion movement. The actress, fondly called Bebo by her fans, proved that motherhood and style are not mutually exclusive. From glamorous red carpet appearances to casual Mumbai outings, Kareena showed expecting mothers across India that pregnancy is the perfect time to celebrate your changing body with fabulous fashion choices.

During her first pregnancy with Taimur in 2016 and later with Jeh in 2020, Kareena continued working, walking ramps, and attending events, all while looking absolutely stunning. Her maternity wardrobe became the talk of the town, inspiring countless mothers-to-be to embrace their pregnancy glow with stylish, comfortable clothing. Let's decode Kareena's maternity style secrets and discover how you can recreate her iconic looks.

The Power of the Maxi Dress

Kareena's go-to choice throughout her pregnancies was the ever-elegant maxi dress. She understood that flowing silhouettes don't just accommodate a growing bump—they celebrate it. From printed bohemian styles to solid-coloured sophisticated numbers, maxi dresses became her signature. The beauty of this style lies in its versatility; you can dress it up with statement jewellery for occasions or keep it casual with flat sandals for everyday wear.

**Maternity Maxi Dress in Soft Jersey Fabric**: Look for breathable, stretchy jersey material maxi dresses that grow with you throughout your pregnancy. Choose empire waist designs that provide comfort under the bust while flowing gracefully over your bump. Opt for darker colours or strategic prints that flatter your figure while keeping you comfortable all day long.

Embracing the Kaftan Culture

Kareena single-handedly brought kaftans back into Indian maternity fashion. These loose, flowing garments are perfect for India's climate and provide ultimate comfort without compromising on style. She was spotted numerous times in beautifully embellished kaftans that looked effortlessly chic whether she was travelling or attending casual gatherings.

**Printed Cotton Kaftan with Embroidery**: A good quality cotton kaftan with delicate embroidery or prints can be your best friend during pregnancy. Look for ones with side slits for easy movement and breathable fabric that keeps you cool. These versatile pieces work beautifully from the second trimester through postpartum, making them an excellent investment.

The Classic White Shirt Dress

One of Kareena's most memorable maternity looks was the crisp white shirt dress she wore multiple times with different accessories. This timeless piece proves that maternity fashion doesn't require a complete wardrobe overhaul. A well-fitted shirt dress in a larger size can see you through your pregnancy with style.

**Oversized Cotton Shirt Dress**: Choose a pure cotton or linen shirt dress that's a few sizes larger than your regular size. The structured collar and button-down design add sophistication, while the loose fit ensures comfort. Pair it with a belt above the bump in early pregnancy or wear it loose later on. This piece will remain a wardrobe staple even after delivery.

Comfortable Yet Stylish Leggings

Kareena proved that comfort and style go hand in hand by pairing stretchy leggings with long kurtas and tunics. This combination is perfect for Indian women who want to maintain their traditional aesthetic while ensuring complete comfort during pregnancy.

**High-Waisted Maternity Leggings**: Invest in good quality, high-waisted maternity leggings with a wide elastic panel that sits comfortably over your bump. Look for thick, opaque fabric that provides adequate coverage and doesn't lose shape after multiple washes. Black is versatile, but don't shy away from navy, grey, or even coloured options to add variety to your wardrobe.

The Statement Accessories

Kareena never let her maternity status stop her from accessorizing boldly. Oversized sunglasses, statement earrings, and designer handbags elevated even her simplest outfits. Remember, when your clothing choices are driven by comfort, accessories become crucial in expressing your personal style.

**Lightweight Statement Jewellery Set**: Since heavy jewellery can be uncomfortable during pregnancy, opt for lightweight oxidised silver or artificial jewellery that makes a statement without the weight. Chunky earrings or layered necklaces can transform a simple maxi dress or kurta into a stylish ensemble worthy of Bebo herself.

Your Kareena-Inspired Maternity Wardrobe Awaits

Pregnancy is a beautiful journey, and like Kareena Kapoor Khan showed us, there's no reason to hide your gorgeous bump under shapeless clothes. Embrace this special time with confidence and style.
