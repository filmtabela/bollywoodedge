Buying Tips

  • Check the earring weight before purchasing—heavy statement earrings can cause discomfort; look for lightweight metals or hollow construction
  • Verify the closure type matches your preference: lever-backs offer security for expensive-looking pieces, while posts work for lighter styles
  • Read measurements carefully as photos can be deceiving; most statement earrings should be 2.5–4 inches long for true impact
  • Choose nickel-free or hypoallergenic metals if you have sensitive ears, especially important for all-day wear at events
