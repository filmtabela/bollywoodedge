Buying Tips

  • Check fabric authenticity by looking for handloom marks or silk certifications; pure fabrics drape better and last longer than synthetic blends
  • Always verify the saree length (standard 5.5-6.3 meters) and blouse piece availability before purchasing online
  • Examine zari work closely—pure zari contains metallic threads while imitation uses golden-colored synthetic threads that may tarnish
  • Read return policies carefully as many sarees are non-returnable once the blouse piece is cut or if the original packaging is damaged
