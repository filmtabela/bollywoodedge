Buying Tips

  • Check interior pockets and compartments—you'll need space for phone, cards, lipstick, and emergency safety pins
  • Test clasps and closures before the big day; magnetic snaps are easier to open than intricate frames when you're in a hurry
  • Consider chain length if purchasing a convertible style—it should hit at hip level when worn as a crossbody
  • White fabric stains easily, so choose materials like satin or leather that can be spot-cleaned, and avoid placing on colored surfaces
