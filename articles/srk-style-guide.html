Shah Rukh Khan has been setting men's style benchmarks in India for three decades. From the open-armed romanticism of the 90s to the sharply suited persona of the 2010s to the evolved, confident ease of his current era — SRK's style has always been aspirational without being unattainable.

The good news: the core of his look is simple, consistent and entirely shoppable on Amazon India.

The SRK Formula

At its heart, Shah Rukh Khan's style is about fit and confidence. He wears well-fitted clothes — not skin-tight, but nothing baggy either. His casual looks favour dark jeans, a clean shirt or well-fitted tee, and a sharp jacket. His formal looks lean into structured suits with open collars. The accessories are minimal: a good watch, sometimes sunglasses, always presence.
