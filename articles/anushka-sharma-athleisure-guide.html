# Anushka Sharma Athleisure Guide: Shop Her Effortlessly Chic Athletic Style

Anushka Sharma has become Bollywood's undisputed queen of athleisure, seamlessly blending comfort with contemporary style. Whether she's spotted at Mumbai airport, heading to her gym, or enjoying casual weekend outings, the actress and producer consistently demonstrates how athletic wear can be both functional and fashionable. Her approach to athleisure perfectly captures the modern woman's lifestyle—always on the go, health-conscious, and style-savvy without trying too hard.

What sets Anushka's athleisure aesthetic apart is her minimalist approach with strategic pops of personality. She favors clean lines, neutral color palettes with occasional bold statements, and always prioritizes quality fabrics that move with the body. From high-waisted leggings paired with cropped hoodies to sleek jogger sets accessorized with designer sneakers, Anushka proves that gym wear doesn't have to sacrifice style for substance. Let's break down her signature athleisure looks and explore how you can recreate this celebrity-approved style with carefully curated pieces available for online shopping.

The Foundation: High-Performance Leggings

The cornerstone of any athleisure wardrobe is a collection of high-quality leggings, and Anushka is frequently photographed in form-fitting, high-waisted styles that offer both compression and comfort. Look for moisture-wicking athletic leggings with four-way stretch technology that provide tummy control while allowing complete freedom of movement. The best options feature seamless construction to prevent chafing during workouts and thick, non-see-through fabric that maintains opacity during squats and stretches. Choose versatile black leggings as your base, then expand to colors like navy, olive green, or charcoal gray. Premium yoga pants with hidden pockets are particularly practical for storing essentials during morning runs or errands. Quality workout leggings serve as the foundation for countless outfit combinations, paired equally well with sports bras, oversized tees, or structured blazers for that elevated athleisure look Anushka frequently sports.

Statement Sports Bras and Crop Tops

Anushka often layers her looks strategically, and the statement sports bra has become her signature piece. Medium to high-support sports bras in bold colors or subtle prints add visual interest while providing necessary support during exercise. Look for racerback designs with adjustable straps and removable padding for versatile styling options. For a fashion-forward approach, choose sports bras with interesting strap details or mesh panels that peek out from under cropped hoodies. Pair these with matching high-waisted leggings to create coordinated athleisure sets that look intentionally styled. Cropped athletic tank tops in breathable fabrics like bamboo or performance cotton offer another layering option, perfect for transitional weather or creating dimension in your outfit. These pieces work beautifully for actual workouts but translate seamlessly to casual coffee runs or weekend brunches when paired with joggers and slides.

The Perfect Oversized Hoodie

Perhaps nothing defines Anushka's off-duty style more than her collection of perfectly oversized hoodies. This athleisure essential strikes the ideal balance between relaxed comfort and put-together polish. Seek out premium cotton-blend hoodies with a slightly boxy fit that drapes elegantly without appearing sloppy. Neutral tones like beige, gray, and black offer maximum versatility, while pastel shades add a softer, feminine touch. The best hoodies feature kangaroo pockets, quality drawstrings, and ribbed cuffs that maintain their shape after multiple washes. Women's athletic hoodies with thumbholes provide extra coverage and a sporty edge, while cropped versions paired with high-waisted bottoms create flattering proportions. Layer these over sports bras with leggings for gym sessions, or dress them up with joggers and white sneakers for airport style that channels Anushka's signature aesthetic.

Elevated Joggers and Track Pants

Moving beyond basic sweatpants, Anushka demonstrates how tailored joggers can elevate your entire athleisure wardrobe. Look for women's jogger pants in structured fabrics with clean lines, tapered legs, and elastic or drawstring waistbands that sit comfortably at the natural waist. Cotton-polyester blend joggers offer durability and shape retention while providing breathability for all-day wear. Side stripe details add athletic authenticity and visual interest, while solid colors maintain versatility for mixing and matching. Choose joggers with functional pockets—both side and back—for practical everyday wear. These athleisure bottoms pair beautifully with fitted crop tops for balanced proportions or oversized hoodies for maximum comfort without sacrificing style.

Premium Athletic Footwear

No athleisure outfit is complete without the right footwear, and Anushka consistently chooses clean, minimalist sneakers that complement rather than overwhelm her looks. White leather sneakers remain the most versatile choice, working with virtually any athleisure combination while maintaining a fresh, contemporary aesthetic. Look for women's training shoes with cushioned soles for actual workouts, or fashion sneakers with sleek silhouettes for lifestyle wear. Slip-on athletic shoes offer convenience for quick trips, while high-top sneakers add edge to basic leggings and hoodie combinations.
