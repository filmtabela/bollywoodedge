# Anushka Sharma Athleisure Guide: Master Bollywood's Queen of Sporty Chic

Anushka Sharma has consistently proven herself as one of Bollywood's most versatile style icons, both on and off the screen. While she dazzles on red carpets in haute couture, it's her effortlessly chic athleisure looks that have truly captured the hearts of fashion enthusiasts worldwide. The actress and producer has mastered the art of combining comfort with style, making her airport looks and gym outfits just as covetable as her designer ensembles. From perfectly fitted leggings to statement sneakers, Anushka's athleisure wardrobe offers endless inspiration for those seeking to elevate their everyday casual wear while maintaining that coveted celebrity aesthetic.

The Foundation: High-Performance Leggings

At the core of Anushka Sharma's athleisure repertoire are high-quality, well-fitted leggings that transition seamlessly from workout sessions to casual outings. She's frequently spotted in high-waisted compression leggings that offer both support and style. Look for moisture-wicking fabric leggings with four-way stretch technology that provide the perfect blend of comfort and functionality. Black remains her go-to colour, though she occasionally opts for navy or charcoal grey for variation. The key is investing in leggings with flatlock seams to prevent chafing and a wide waistband that doesn't dig in during movement. These essential pieces form the building blocks of any athleisure wardrobe and pair beautifully with everything from cropped hoodies to oversized t-shirts.

Elevate with Statement Sports Bras

Anushka frequently showcases her toned physique in stylish sports bras that double as crop tops. Rather than hiding them under layers, she embraces the athleisure trend of making sports bras the focal point of her outfit. Medium to high-impact sports bras with adjustable straps and removable padding offer versatility for both workouts and casual wear. Look for options with interesting back detailing – strappy designs or mesh panels add visual interest when layering with sheer fabrics or wearing solo. Neutral colours like white, black, and grey provide maximum versatility, though a pop of colour in shades like burgundy or forest green can add personality to your athleisure collection. The right sports bra should offer excellent support while making you feel confident enough to wear it as outerwear.

The Perfect Oversized Hoodie

No athleisure wardrobe inspired by Anushka Sharma would be complete without an oversized hoodie. She's mastered the art of the relaxed, slouchy hoodie paired with fitted bottoms for that perfectly balanced silhouette. Opt for premium cotton blend hoodies with a brushed fleece interior for ultimate comfort. Neutral tones work best for versatility, though Anushka has been spotted in pastel shades during off-duty moments. The ideal oversized hoodie should hit at mid-thigh length and feature a relaxed fit through the body with dropped shoulders. Look for details like thumbholes in the cuffs or kangaroo pockets that add functionality. This piece works wonderfully for travel days, coffee runs, or layering during cooler months while maintaining that celebrity-inspired casual sophistication.

Sneakers That Make a Statement

Anushka's footwear choices demonstrate that the right sneakers can elevate any athleisure outfit from basic to outstanding. She gravitates toward chunky white sneakers that offer both comfort and style credentials. Classic leather sneakers in pristine white remain the most versatile option, working with virtually any colour combination in your wardrobe. For those seeking extra support during workouts, consider cushioned running shoes with breathable mesh uppers and responsive sole technology. The beauty of investing in quality sneakers is their dual functionality – they perform during exercise while looking fashion-forward for everyday wear. Anushka often keeps her sneakers immaculately clean, proving that well-maintained footwear is essential to achieving that polished celebrity look.

Complete the Look: Accessories and Outerwear

The finishing touches make all the difference in achieving Anushka's signature athleisure aesthetic. A structured backpack in leather or premium nylon offers practicality without sacrificing style, perfect for carrying essentials to the gym or running errands. Aviator sunglasses add instant glamour to even the most casual ensemble, while a simple cap can transform your look on days when you want to channel her airport style. For transitional weather, a lightweight bomber jacket or windbreaker in a neutral shade provides the perfect layering piece.
