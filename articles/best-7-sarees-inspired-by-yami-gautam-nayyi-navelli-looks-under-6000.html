Buying Tips

  • Check fabric composition carefully—pure silk and handloom sarees should mention certification or loom type in the description
  • Look for sarees with fall and pico stitching already done, or budget an additional ₹300–500 for tailoring
  • Read reviews specifically about colour accuracy, as screen displays often differ from actual fabric shades
  • Verify return policies for sarees, especially for unstitched items, as some sellers don't accept returns on traditional wear
