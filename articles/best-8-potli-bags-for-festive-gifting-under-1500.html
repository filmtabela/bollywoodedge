Buying Tips

  • Check fabric quality and lining—a proper lining prevents snagging and protects contents from embroidery threads or embellishments
  • Ensure drawstrings or clasps function smoothly and are securely attached, as these are stress points that determine longevity
  • For bulk gifting, order one sample first to verify colour accuracy and craftsmanship before purchasing larger quantities
  • Store potlis in breathable cloth bags between uses to prevent fabric discolouration and keep embellishments intact
