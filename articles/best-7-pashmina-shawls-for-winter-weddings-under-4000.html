Buying Tips

  • Check the fabric blend carefully—pure pashmina is rare under ₹4,000; look for wool-pashmina or cashmere blends that offer good warmth and softness at this price point
  • Test the weave density by holding the shawl up to light; tighter weaves provide better warmth and durability for multiple wears throughout wedding season
  • Verify return policies before purchase, especially for embroidered or embellished pieces where color and detailing may appear different from photos
  • Store pashmina shawls folded (never hung) in breathable cotton bags with cedar balls to prevent moth damage between wedding seasons
