Buying Tips

  • Check the fabric composition carefully—natural blends like cotton-silk or rayon offer better breathability than pure synthetic materials for long wear during celebrations
  • Always refer to the size chart provided by the seller as Anarkali sizing varies; measure your bust, waist and desired length before ordering
  • Examine product images for stitching quality around the yoke, sleeves and hemline—look for neat finishing and proper lining details
  • Verify the return and exchange policy before purchase, especially for online orders, as colour and fit can sometimes differ from expectations
