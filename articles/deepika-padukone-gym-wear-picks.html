Whether she's spotted outside her Mumbai gym or sharing workout snippets on social media, the Bollywood diva proves that athletic wear can be both functional and fashionable. Her gym wardrobe strikes the perfect balance between comfort and style, making her a go-to inspiration for women who want to look chic whilst breaking a sweat.

High-Waisted Compression Leggings

Deepika is frequently spotted in sleek, high-waisted leggings that offer both support and style. These form-fitting bottoms have become a staple in her gym bag for good reason. High-waisted compression leggings provide excellent tummy control whilst allowing complete freedom of movement during yoga, pilates, or strength training sessions.

Look for leggings crafted from moisture-wicking fabric with four-way stretch technology. The high-rise design not only flatters your silhouette but also stays in place during intense workouts, eliminating the need for constant adjustments. Opt for classic black for versatility, or choose navy blue or burgundy to add subtle colour to your gym wardrobe. These leggings pair beautifully with crop tops or oversized t-shirts, just like Deepika prefers.

Seamless Sports Bra Sets

The actress understands the importance of proper support, and her collection of sports bras reflects this knowledge. Seamless sports bra sets have become increasingly popular, offering medium to high impact support without uncomfortable underwires or clasps. Deepika often coordinates her sports bras with matching leggings for a polished, put-together appearance.

Choose sports bras with removable padding and adjustable straps for customised comfort. The seamless construction minimises chafing during cardio sessions, whilst the breathable fabric keeps you cool. Neutral tones like grey, beige, and white work wonderfully as basics, but don't shy away from vibrant colours like coral or mint green to channel Deepika's confident style. These coordinated sets make getting dressed for the gym effortless and ensure you always look camera-ready.

Lightweight Training Jackets

Even during Mumbai's warm weather, Deepika is often seen layering with lightweight training jackets. These versatile pieces are perfect for transitioning from outdoors to air-conditioned gyms or for post-workout errands. Her preference leans towards zip-up styles with thumb holes and sleek silhouettes.

Select jackets made from quick-dry polyester or nylon blends that offer breathability without bulk. Features like zippered pockets are practical for storing your phone and keys, whilst reflective details add a sporty edge. Classic colours like black and grey offer maximum versatility, though dusty pink or olive green can add personality to your athleisure collection. A good training jacket elevates your entire gym outfit whilst providing functional benefits.

Cushioned Running Shoes

Footwear is where Deepika never compromises, frequently investing in quality running shoes that provide proper support. Whilst she's been spotted in premium brands, there are excellent alternatives available that offer similar benefits without the hefty price tag. Look for running shoes with adequate cushioning, particularly if you enjoy cardio workouts or running.

Prioritise shoes with breathable mesh uppers, responsive midsoles, and good arch support. The right pair should feel comfortable from the first wear and provide stability during lateral movements. White or light grey sneakers maintain a clean, minimalist aesthetic that Deepika favours, whilst still offering the technical features needed for effective workouts. Remember, proper footwear is an investment in your fitness journey and injury prevention.

Minimalist Gym Bag

Completing your Bollywood-inspired gym wardrobe is a sleek, functional gym bag. Deepika typically carries compact duffel bags or backpacks that accommodate essentials without appearing bulky. A well-designed gym bag should have separate compartments for shoes, a water bottle, and sweaty clothes.

Choose bags made from water-resistant materials with adjustable straps for comfortable carrying. Neutral colours like black, navy, or taupe work with any outfit, whilst rose gold or burgundy hardware adds a feminine touch. A dedicated pocket for valuables keeps your belongings secure and organised, making your gym transitions seamless.
