# Paul Mescal's Simple Masculine Style: A Guide to Effortless Men's Fashion

Paul Mescal has rapidly become one of Hollywood's most compelling style icons, proving that understated masculinity trumps flashy trends every time. The Irish actor, who captured hearts in "Normal People" and earned critical acclaim for his role in "Aftersun," brings the same thoughtful minimalism to his wardrobe that he does to his performances. His approach to men's fashion is refreshingly unpretentious—think quality basics, perfect proportions, and a colour palette that wouldn't look out of place in a Scandi design magazine. For style-conscious men seeking to elevate their everyday look without the fuss, Mescal's aesthetic offers the perfect blueprint.

The Foundation: Premium Basic Tees and Tanks

At the heart of Paul Mescal's signature look lies an arsenal of impeccably fitted t-shirts and tank tops. The actor has been photographed countless times in simple white tees and classic tank tops that showcase his athletic build without trying too hard. The key is choosing premium cotton basics that drape well and maintain their shape after multiple washes.

When shopping online for similar pieces, look for heavyweight cotton t-shirts in neutral tones—white, black, navy, and grey. Quality construction makes all the difference between looking casually cool and simply underdressed. A well-fitted crew neck tee in organic cotton serves as the ultimate versatile wardrobe staple, easily transitioning from coffee runs to casual evening drinks. Pack of basic tees in various neutral colours offers excellent value and ensures you always have this foundation piece ready.

Tailored Shorts: The Mescal Summer Uniform

Perhaps nothing defines Paul Mescal's off-duty style more than his preference for tailored shorts. Forget baggy cargo shorts or overly technical athletic wear—Mescal opts for structured, shorter-cut styles that hit above the knee and feature clean lines. This creates a silhouette that's simultaneously relaxed and refined.

Men's chino shorts in neutral colours like olive, tan, or navy capture this aesthetic perfectly. Look for options with a flat front and minimal detailing. The shorter inseam (around 5-7 inches) that Mescal favours creates a more contemporary, European-inspired proportion. These work brilliantly with the basic tees mentioned above and simple trainers for an effortlessly put-together summer look.

Chunky Chain Necklaces: Subtle Statement Accessories

While Mescal's style skews minimalist, he's not afraid of accessories—specifically, chunky silver chain necklaces. This single piece of jewellery adds just enough edge to his otherwise pared-back outfits, demonstrating how one well-chosen accessory can elevate an entire look.

A sterling silver chain necklace in a medium-weight design strikes the right balance between noticeable and understated. When browsing men's jewellery online, look for chains that sit just below the collarbone and have a slightly oxidized or matte finish rather than high polish for that lived-in quality that characterizes Mescal's approach to style.

Classic White Trainers: The Everyday Sneaker

No contemporary style guide would be complete without addressing footwear, and Paul Mescal consistently turns to clean white trainers as his go-to choice. This classic shoe option works with virtually everything in his wardrobe—shorts, jeans, or tailored trousers—making it an intelligent investment piece.

Men's white leather sneakers with minimal branding capture this aesthetic best. Look for pairs with clean lines, quality leather uppers, and comfortable cushioning for all-day wear. The beauty of this style staple is its versatility; the same shoes that look great with casual weekend wear transition seamlessly to smart-casual settings when paired with well-fitted trousers.

Oversized Linen Shirts: Elevated Beach Casual

For slightly more dressed-up occasions or warm weather styling, Mescal often reaches for oversized linen shirts in natural tones. These breathable, textured pieces add sophistication while maintaining the relaxed vibe that defines his personal style.

When shopping for men's linen shirts, prioritize natural fibres in colours like white, sand, or pale blue. The slightly oversized fit is crucial—too tight and you lose that effortless quality; too baggy and you risk looking sloppy. These work wonderfully layered over tank tops or worn unbuttoned as a lightweight jacket.
