His look centres on timeless pieces in neutral tones—think perfectly fitted white t-shirts, well-cut chinos, and versatile knitwear. The Mescal Short: Embracing Proportions

Perhaps no single item has become more synonymous with Paul Mescal than his preference for shorter inseam shorts. Challenging conventional masculine fashion norms, he's championed a return to 1970s-style proportions that show confidence and body positivity.

**Men's Chino Shorts with 5-7 Inch Inseam**: To achieve the Mescal short aesthetic, seek out tailored chino shorts in khaki, navy, or olive with an inseam that hits mid-thigh. These men's casual shorts should fit comfortably at the waist without being too tight, creating a clean silhouette that elongates the leg. Pair them with simple trainers or leather sandals and a tucked-in tee for that effortlessly cool Irish summer look.

Elevated Outerwear Choices

While Mescal keeps his base layers simple, he elevates outfits through considered outerwear selections. His approach favours classic silhouettes in quality materials over flashy statement pieces.

**Wool-Blend Overcoat**: For cooler months, a camel or charcoal wool-blend overcoat captures Mescal's refined sensibility. Choose a length that hits just above the knee with a slim but not skinny fit through the body. These men's dress coats instantly elevate even the simplest jeans-and-sweater combination, perfect for evening events or professional settings.

**Classic Denim Jacket**: The timeless denim jacket appears regularly in Mescal's off-duty rotation. Opt for medium-wash denim in a slightly fitted cut that allows for layering. This men's casual jacket works with everything from shorts to tailored trousers, embodying the versatile functionality that defines his aesthetic.

Completing the Look: Footwear and Accessories

Mescal's minimalist approach extends to accessories, where he favors quality over quantity. Simple leather shoes, minimal jewellery, and functional bags complete his outfits without overwhelming them.
