Buying Tips

  • Check velvet composition—cotton or silk blends drape better and feel more luxurious than synthetic-only options, though polyester velvet is easier to care for
  • Order one size up if between sizes, as velvet doesn't have much stretch and can feel restrictive across the bust and hips
  • Look for lined dresses to prevent the velvet from clinging uncomfortably and to ensure the dress hangs properly without transparency
  • Confirm the return policy before purchasing since velvet shows wear quickly—you want the option to return if the fabric looks crushed or damaged upon arrival
