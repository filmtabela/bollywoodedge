Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

BollywoodEdge (imraankhan.in) publishes fashion, beauty and shopping guides. This page explains what information is involved when you use the site.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or payment details. The site has no sign-up or contact forms.

Like most websites, our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) automatically processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Affiliate links

BollywoodEdge is a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme (Amazon.in). When you click a product link and buy on Amazon, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon may use cookies to record that you arrived from our site. Your purchase is handled entirely by Amazon under Amazon's privacy notice; we never see your name, address or order details.

Cookies

BollywoodEdge itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third parties you interact with through our links (such as Amazon or Pinterest) may set their own cookies under their policies.

Images and embedded content

Photos are provided by Pexels and are loaded from Pexels' servers. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Pinterest

We share our own articles to our BollywoodEdge Pinterest account. We use Pinterest's tools only to publish to our own account and do not access or store data about other Pinterest users.

Children

The site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, message us on Pinterest at pinterest.com/bollywoodedge.
