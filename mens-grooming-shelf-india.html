My Actual Grooming Shelf: 9 Products Every Indian Man Over 40 Needs

By Imran Ahmed Khan · 8 min read · June 2026

I'm 48. And for most of my 40s I had no grooming routine — just a bar of soap, whatever shampoo was in the bathroom, and the odd splash of cologne on good days. Sound familiar? Most Indian men my age are in exactly the same place. Nobody ever taught us this stuff. We watched our fathers use Godrej hair dye and Pond's cold cream and figured that was it. It isn't. Here's everything actually on my shelf right now — products I've personally bought, tested, and kept rebuying.

"The grooming market talks to 25-year-olds. Nobody talks to us — the 40-plus Indian man who wants to look sharp, command respect, and not smell like his grandfather's medicine cabinet."

Why This Matters More at 40+

After 40, your skin loses moisture faster, pigmentation becomes more visible, hair gets thinner or greyer, and you have less time and patience for complicated routines. The good news: a simple, consistent 5-minute routine with the right products fixes 80% of the visible aging. You don't need 12 steps. You need 5 things that actually work for Indian skin.

The 5-Minute Morning Routine

This is all you need. Takes less time than scrolling Instagram:

1. Wash face with mCaffeine Coffee Face Wash (60 seconds)
2. Apply Nivea Men SPF moisturiser (30 seconds)
3. Two drops beard oil, work through beard (30 seconds)
4. Hair clay — warm between palms, run through hair (60 seconds)
5. SPF lip balm (5 seconds)
6. Two sprays of your EDP on neck and wrists (10 seconds)

That's it. 5 minutes. You walk out looking intentional, smelling good, and five years younger. Do this every day for 90 days and the cumulative effect is dramatic.

On Pigmentation (Important for 40+ Indian Men) Dark spots, uneven tone, and pigmentation are extremely common for Indian men over 40 — especially on the face, forehead, and around the nose. Consistent sunscreen use prevents new pigmentation. For existing spots, look for serums with Vitamin C or Niacinamide. These take 8–12 weeks to work but they do work.
